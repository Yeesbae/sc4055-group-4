# Limitations and unsuccessful diagnostics

1. All historical periods through October 2026 were already available to earlier exploration. Nested rolling origins prevent algorithmic future leakage, but cannot undo researcher-level historical inspection. No untouched prospective evaluation exists.
2. Official current-vintage daily closes are available; a point-in-time vintage database is not. Historical revisions and real-time dissemination availability cannot be independently ruled out. This is a retrospective model study.
3. Every generated history is reachable under smoothed probabilities. This does not imply empirical support: three-level higher orders have unobserved histories. Exact equality of some smoothed history laws permits an exact quotient; it does not demonstrate equal laws in financial reality.
4. The recorded adaptive selector sometimes chooses IID after quiet validation years. Its subsequent event Brier is worse than fixed first order. This failure is retained; no post-hoc selector repair is presented as original validation.
5. The 0.001 practical Brier tie tolerance and minimum context count of 25 are analyst engineering choices, not finance standards. Tightening the tolerance changes the final model; sensitivity is exported.
6. Binary order 2/3 improve one-step scores but have primary-event confidence intervals spanning zero against order 1. Additional history is not established as useful for that event.
7. Direct regression on continuous current log-VIX and its latest change improves event scores. The proposed finite-state model sacrifices financial information for a small explicit generator. It is not the strongest demonstrated forecaster.
8. The three-level order-3 zero-phase Gram is singular before minimization. Its 24-state quotient still has a numerically unresolved full-rank Cholesky factorization. No arbitrary spectral cutoff or pseudoinverse is used to claim an exact instrument. This large sparse candidate is not advanced.
9. Rank mod a prime gives exact rational lower bounds. A nonzero square determinant proves full rational rank; a deficient modular rank does not prove exact rank deficiency. The binary order-3 rank-eight certificate excludes a fixed one-qubit instrument, but is not a no-compression theorem for all larger quantum dimensions.
10. The score bootstrap conditions on the fitted historical forecasting procedure. It does not rerun model selection within every score replicate. Parameter block refits are a separate analysis; both remain sensitive to nonstationarity and limited independent stress episodes.
11. Initial streaming compression produced a truncated forecast ledger caught by the readback test. The exporter now serializes, compresses and verifies a byte-for-byte decompression before publishing. The delivered ledger and final tests pass. No statistical observations or estimates were deleted to repair this software issue.
12. No real quantum circuit, QAE production run, phase optimization, lossy instrument training, economic utility backtest, returns or default model is implemented here. Gate counts and hardware performance remain untested.
